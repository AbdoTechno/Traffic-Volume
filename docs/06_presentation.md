## Committee Presentation Structure (15 Minutes)

1. **Slide 1:** Title & Team Information (Cover slide, student names, supervisor, track)
2. **Slide 2:** Executive Agenda (Presentation outline and flow)
3. **Slide 3:** Problem Statement & Motivation (Congestion on I-94, economic impact, emissions)
4. **Slide 4:** Project Objectives & Scope (Forecasting goals, quantitative targets, scope)
5. **Slide 5:** Dataset Overview (UCI repository source, 48,204 rows, 8 features + target)
6. **Slide 6:** Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Anomaly removal, 0K correction, datetime parsing)
7. **Slide 7:** EDA - Temporal Dynamics (Morning/evening rush hours, weekday vs. weekend)
8. **Slide 8:** EDA - Weather Impact (Rainfall, snowfall, cloud cover, and temperature effects)
9. **Slide 9:** Feature Engineering (Cyclical Sine/Cosine encoding, time-series splitting)
10. **Slide 10:** Machine Learning Pipeline (Candidate models: Ridge, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM)
11. **Slide 11:** Experimental Results & Benchmarks (Metrics comparison table: R2, MAE, RMSE)
12. **Slide 12:** Feature Importance & Explainability (SHAP / Feature importance analysis)
13. **Slide 13:** Business Value & Deployment (Smart traffic management, routing, maintenance planning)
14. **Slide 14:** Challenges & Limitations (Westbound-only data, rare weather events, sensor gaps)
15. **Slide 15:** Conclusion & Future Roadmap (Key takeaways, deep learning, real-time API integration)
16. **Slide 16:** Q&A / Committee Discussion (Open floor for questions and answers)

## Presentation Guidelines
- Time Management: Allocate approximately 1 minute per slide (total 10-15 minutes).
- Visual Emphasis: Prioritize plots, charts, and comparison tables over dense text paragraphs.
- Committee Defense: Review the expected committee questions and defense strategies detailed in the template file.
