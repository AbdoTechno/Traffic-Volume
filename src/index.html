Key Insights

The Three Big Drivers of Traffic

Understanding what moves interstate volume from 400 to 7,000+ vehicles per hour.

1. Time of Day (Rush Hours)

Explains 80%+ of volume changes. Sharp twin peaks on weekdays: 7–9 AM morning commute and 4–6 PM evening return.

2. Weather & Winter Snow

Minnesota snowstorms and freezing rain slow throughput by up to 35%, while clear warm days maintain max flow.

3. Holidays & Weekends

Major holidays suppress regular morning peaks, turning busy workdays into gentle afternoon curves.
