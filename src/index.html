Key insights

Three drivers move most of the volume

What separates 400 vehicles an hour from 7,000+.

Time of day

Explains most of the variance on its own. Weekdays show two sharp peaks: 7–9 AM inbound, 4–6 PM outbound.

Weather & winter snow

Heavy snow and freezing rain cut throughput by up to a third. Clear, mild days hold peak flow the longest.

Holidays & weekends

Holidays flatten the morning peak entirely, turning a workday commute pattern into a gentle afternoon curve.
